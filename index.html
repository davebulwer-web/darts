Oche Legends

501, 301 and Round the Clock
New game
Hand speed

The hand never stops. Steer it with the arrow keys or WASD (the hand always moves diagonally: up or down flips its vertical direction, left or right flips its horizontal direction) and throw with Space. Tap the board to throw on a touch screen. Pick 501, 301 or Round the Clock in the menu. In 501 and 301, finish exactly on zero with a double or the bull; going below zero or leaving 1 is a bust, and the blackboard suggests a checkout once you can finish. In Round the Clock, hit 1 to 20 in order, then the bull. Beat each opponent to unlock the next, or enter the tournament as any of the 16 players and win four knockout matches to take the trophy. Keys: Esc menu, 1 vs computer, 2 two players, 3 tournament, P practice, M mute.
